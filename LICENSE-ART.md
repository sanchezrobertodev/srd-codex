## How they were made

Every picture was generated with OpenAI's GPT Image model (through the Codex CLI) from a written house-style brief ("ink and watercolour") and a per-entry subject note, then reviewed by hand before release. Each file's prompt, model and reviewer are recorded in `packages/assets/licences.json` (the `ai` field), and the briefs are in `packages/assets/art/art.json`.

Copyright protection for AI-generated images varies by country and may be limited. The CC BY 4.0 grant above applies to whatever rights the author holds in them.
